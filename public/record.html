Do not use Bluetooth headphones. Activating a Bluetooth mic switches the device to the HFP profile — 8–16 kHz mono, built for phone calls. It often fails outright, and when it works the audio is degraded enough to inflate the word error rate, so we would be measuring the microphone rather than the speech recogniser. Pick the built-in mic instead.
